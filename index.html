Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of our FAQs. If you have any other questions you'd like answered please feel free to email us.

What is Bookmark?

Bookmark is a simple and efficient bookmark manager that allows you to organize and access your favourite websites with ease. It provides a clean and user-friendly interface, making it easy to save, categorize, and manage your bookmarks across different devices. With features like intelligent search and easy sharing, Bookmark helps you stay organized and quickly find the websites you need, whether you're at home or on the go.

How can I request a new browser?

If you would like to request a new browser to be supported by Bookmark, please feel free to contact our support team. You can reach us via email at support@bookmark.com. We value your feedback and will consider adding support for additional browsers based on user demand. Please include the name of the browser you would like to see supported and any specific features you would like to have in that browser.

Is there a mobile app?

Currently, Bookmark does not have a dedicated mobile app. However, our web application is fully responsive and can be accessed on mobile devices through a web browser. We are continuously working on improving our services and may consider developing a mobile app in the future based on user demand.

What about other Chromium browsers?

Bookmark is designed to work with all Chromium-based browsers, which means that it should be compatible with browsers such as Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Vivaldi. If you are using a Chromium-based browser and encounter any issues with Bookmark, please don't hesitate to contact our support team at support@bookmark.com. We are committed to providing a seamless experience for all users, regardless of their choice of browser.
